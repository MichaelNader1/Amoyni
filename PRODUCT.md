# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Amoyni serves church youth who check attendance, follow points and streaks, and join community activities, plus administrators who manage meetings, attendance rewards, raffles, referrals, donations, and reporting. The primary admin workflow is completing operational tasks quickly in an Arabic, RTL interface on desktop or mobile.

## Product Purpose

Provide one dependable web application for church youth engagement and administration. Success means administrators can run meetings and rewards without data mistakes while youth can participate with minimal friction.

## Brand Personality

Youthful, energetic, and trustworthy. The product should feel encouraging and lively without becoming childish, noisy, or difficult to operate.

## Anti-references

Avoid dense enterprise interfaces that obscure the next action, decorative gaming UI that competes with operational content, and unfamiliar controls where standard form and navigation patterns work better.

## Design Principles

- Make the current task and next action immediately clear.
- Preserve familiar controls and consistent behavior across admin screens.
- Prevent operational mistakes with early, specific feedback.
- Keep core workflows comfortable on both phones and desktop screens.
- Use energy for meaningful feedback while keeping data-entry surfaces calm.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal WCAG target is documented. Preserve semantic labels, keyboard-accessible native controls, visible focus states, readable contrast, RTL layout, touch-sized actions, responsive reflow, and reduced-motion behavior where motion is introduced.
